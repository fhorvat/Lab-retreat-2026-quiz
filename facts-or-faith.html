Group Meeting · Diagnostic Binary judgement · 11 items

Facts, or
Faith?

A short diagnostic on what current evidence does — and does not — support. Each claim sounds plausible. Only some of them are real.

Format Two choices Items 11 Duration ~10 min
For each statement, decide whether the claim is supported by current peer-reviewed evidence (Facts) or whether it merely sounds plausible but lacks robust backing (Faith). No partial credit. No hedging.
